Picture Perfect Indicators

Interactive Plotly charts with OHLC candlesticks

What you’ll see

Numba implementations shown on SPY daily candles, Gold COT reports, and weekly SPY relative to Gold. Price charts focus on 2023 onward; COT shows its available history.

1 · Commercial Proxy Index

8 bar price proxy: SMA(open − close, 8) / SMA(TR, 8) × 50 + 50. Above 50 indicates downward intrabar bias.

Trading use: Follow commercial bias: buy pullbacks in uptrends, sell rallies in downtrends.

2 · Large Trader Proxy Index

MMA(close − close[8], 40) / MMA(TR, 40) × 50 + 50. Wilder style average; readings center near 50.

Trading use: Follow proxy direction; use an average cross to time entries and extremes to watch for exhaustion.

3 · COT Index

Gold commercial net normalized over 13 reports. Dates reflect report availability; Gold candles provide price context.

Trading use: Buy commercial accumulation in uptrends; sell distribution in downtrends; time entries separately.

4 · Williams Valuation Index

Weekly SPY relative to Gold; EMA periods 2/22, normalized over 156 weeks. The reference market changes the readings.

Trading use: Look long below ~15 in a strong uptrend and short above ~75 in a strong downtrend.

5 · Williams POIV AD

Requires open interest. Local SPY data has OHLCV only, so this chart shows candles and volume; POIV is not calculated.

Trading use: Bullish divergence: price makes a low POIV misses; bearish: price makes a high it misses.

6 · Blast Off!

One bar: abs(close − open) / true range × 100.

Trading use: Low readings flag contraction and a possible larger open-to-close move in the next day or two.

7 · WillTrend

66 bar average range, multiplier 2.236 (local implementation). Candles show price with the trend line overlaid.

Trading use: Follow price above/below the ratcheting line; treat a flip as exit or reversal.

Ringed swing highs and lows

Down arrows mark confirmed ringed highs; up arrows mark confirmed ringed lows. Each point is confirmed by the bar to its right, and an adjacent inside bar disqualifies it.

Trading use: After reversal, buy the first higher ringed low or sell the first lower ringed high; stop past swing.

Reading the charts

These are illustrations, not trade signals. COT uses report availability dates; WVI depends on its reference market; POIV requires open interest.

Trading notes summarize Larry Williams’ Picture Perfect Trading Manual (2007), Sections 2–3 and 5–7.
